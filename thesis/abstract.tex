A measurement of the Higgs boson production cross section in associated \wh{} and \zh{} production using $H \to WW^{*} \to \ell\nu\ell\nu$ decays is presented. This thesis uses proton-proton collision data collected by the ATLAS detector at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) between 2022 and 2024, corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 165.4~fb$^{-1}$ at a center-of-mass energy of $\sqrt{s} = 13.6$~TeV. Only the \wh{} channel is unblinded at the time of writing, and therefore the observed results reported here refer exclusively to \wh{} production. The product of the \hww{} branching fraction, the leptonic branching fraction of the $W$ boson, and the \wh{} production cross section is measured to be $0.26^{+0.13}_{-0.11}(\text{stat.})\pm{0.05}(\text{sys.})$ pb, in agreement with the Standard Model prediction of $0.311\pm0.006$ pb. Additionally, The 2022-2024 results are interpreted within the Simplified Template Cross Sections (STXS) framework.
