This section presents the expected and observed results for the STXS analysis performed in this thesis for the \wh{} \zdom{} and \zdep{} categories, the combined \wh{} channel and the \zh{} channel results. There is no combination with the Run~2 and Run~3 STXS analyses due to the change in center-of-mass energy. This change results in a different expected \ptv{} distribution that cannot be easily accounted for. The results are presented for three different binnings of \ptv{}, $\ptv < 75$~GeV, $75 \leq \ptv < 150$~GeV, and $\ptv \geq 150$~GeV.
