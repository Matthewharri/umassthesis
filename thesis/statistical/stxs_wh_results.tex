In contrast to the inclusive analysis which measures a single POI, in the STXS analysis three POIs are measured per SR category for each \ptv{} bin. From the fit, the expected signal strengths in the \zdom{} SR for each \ptv{} bin, listed in order of $\ptv < 75$~GeV, $75 \leq \ptv < 150$~GeV, and $\ptv \geq 150$~GeV, are found to be $\mu = 1.01^{+1.97}_{-1.79}$, $\mu = 1.01^{+2.14}_{-1.96}$ and $\mu = 1.00^{+1.41}_{-1.24}$, and in the $Z$-depleted channel, these are found to be $\mu = 1.01^{+1.5}_{-1.3}$, $\mu = 1.02^{+1.6}_{-1.3}$ and $\mu = 1.01^{+1.3}_{-1.0}$. The corresponding observed values for the \zdom{} SR are $\mu = 1.33^{+2.1}_{-1.9}$, $\mu = -0.17^{+2.2}_{-2.0}$ and $\mu = 0.21^{+1.5}_{-1.4}$, and for the \zdep{} SR are found to be $\mu = 1.95^{+1.6}_{-1.4}$, $\mu = 0.75^{+1.5}_{-1.3}$ and $\mu = 0.92^{+1.3}_{-1.0}$. In the combined \wh{} channel, the expected $\mu$ values are found to be $\mu = 1.01^{+1.2}_{-1.1}$, $\mu = 1.02^{+1.3}_{-1.1}$, and $\mu = 1.00^{+1.0}_{-0.8}$, and the observed values are found to be $\mu = 1.42^{+1.3}_{-1.1}$, $\mu = 0.54^{+1.2}_{-1.0}$ and $\mu = 0.67^{+1.0}_{-0.8}$.

The impact of statistical and systematic uncertainties on the STXS \wh{} measurement is assessed through the pulls (Appendix~\ref{app:wh_stxs_pull_plots}), the correlations between uncertainties (Appendix~\ref{app:wh_stxs_correlation_plots}), and the systematic ranking plots (Appendix~\ref{app:wh_stxs_ranking_plots}). The NFs for the $WZ$ CR's are found to be $\text{NF}_{WZ~\text{0 Jets}} = 0.91^{+0.10}_{-0.10}$, $\text{NF}_{WZ~\text{1 Jet}} = 0.92^{+0.12}_{-0.12}$, and $\text{NF}_{WZ~\text{2+ Jets}} = 0.88^{+0.16}_{-0.16}$. The NF for the $WWW$ CR is found to be $\text{NF}_{WWW \text{ CR}} = 1.81^{+0.68}_{-0.68}$.
