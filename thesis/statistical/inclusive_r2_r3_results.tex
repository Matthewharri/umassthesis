The results presented here are those resulting from the combination of the Run~2 and Run~3 analyses. Like the previous sections, this result requires the \zh{} channel, and thus only the expected results are reported. The signal strength for this combination is found to have an expected value of $\mu = 1.0^{+0.19}_{-0.19}$. A profile likelihood scan of the signal strength can be seen in Figure~\ref{fig:r2_r3_likelihood_scan}.

This combination evaluates to an expected significance of $6.68\sigma$. As expected, this channel is dominated by statistical uncertainties.
