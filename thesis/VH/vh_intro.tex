This thesis focuses on Higgs boson production in association with a $W$ or $Z$ boson, respectively denoted as \wh{} and \zh{}, and collectively referred to as \VH{}. The VH process provides direct access to the Higgs boson couplings to weak bosons. Particularly interesting is the \wh{} production with \hww{} decay, where the Higgs boson couples only to $W$ bosons at both production and decay vertices, and thus yields a direct measurement of the coupling between Higgs and $W$ bosons without any assumption about Higgs couplings to other particles.

Previous analyses have been performed in this channel, with the most recent being a result from the ATLAS collaboration using the data from 2015--2018, with a luminosity of 140 fb$^{-1}$ at $\com = 13$ TeV~\cite{ATLAS:2025abg}. That analysis measured the \VH{} cross section with a significance of 4.5~$\sigma$ over the background-only hypothesis. The CMS collaboration also released a measurement of the \VH{} production cross section in the \hww{} decay channel using 137 fb$^{-1}$ of data at $\com = 13$ TeV, with a significance of 4.7~$\sigma$~\cite{CMS:2021ixs}.

In this thesis, a measurement of the corresponding inclusive production cross section with the \hww{} decay will be performed using $pp$ collisions at $\com = 13.6$ TeV, where an increase of approximately 7\% in the cross section is expected relative to Run~2 due to the higher center-of-mass energy. Additionally, a differential cross-section measurement will be performed as a function of the transverse momentum of the associated vector boson, \ptv{}. Three \ptv{} bins will be used: $0 < \ptv < 75$, $75 < \ptv < 150$, and $\ptv > 150$ GeV. Finally, the Run 2 and Run 3 measurements will be combined to extract the inclusive \VH{} cross section with \hww{} decay.

The analysis covered in this thesis features events with three (3$\ell$) or four (4$\ell$) charged leptons (electrons or muons) in the final state for the \wh{} and \zh{} channels, respectively. In the previous Run 2 analysis, the $2\ell$ channel was also included, but was found to be systematically limited and expected to not provide much additional sensitivity in this analysis and is thus not included. Leptonic decays of $\tau$ leptons originating from the Higgs boson or associated vector boson are considered as signal since the corresponding electrons or muons will be reconstructed, while hadronically decaying $\tau$ leptons are considered as background. Additionally, events from all other Higgs production mechanisms are treated as background. 

The 3$\ell$ channel targets \wh{} production, and is separated into two orthogonal categories: the \zdom{} category contains events with at least one same-flavor opposite-sign (SFOS) lepton pair, and the \zdep{} category contains no such SFOS lepton pairs. Similarly, the 4$\ell$ channel is split into two categories: one with a single SFOS lepton pair, and one with two SFOS lepton pairs.

The analysis is conducted in a fully blinded manner to avoid bias in the signal regions. Only the control regions, which are dominated by background processes and used for validation of background modeling, are examined during development. All expected results including statistical interpretations, are obtained prior to requesting and receiving approval for unblinding.

The rest of this thesis will primarily focus on the 3$\ell$ channel, with a brief summary of the 4$\ell$ channel.
