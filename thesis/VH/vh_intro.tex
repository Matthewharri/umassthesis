This thesis focuses on Higgs boson production with a $W$ or $Z$ boson, respectively denoted as \wh{} and \zh{}, and collectively referred to as \VH{}, and provides direct access to the Higgs boson couplings to weak bosons. Particularly interesting is the \wh{} decay mode with \hww{} decay, where the Higgs boson couples only to $W$ bosons at both production and decay vertices. 

Previous analyses have been performed in this channel, with the most recent being a result from the ATLAS collaboration using the data from 2015--2018, with a luminosity of 140 fb$^{-1}$ at $\com = 13$ TeV~\cite{ATLAS:2025abg}. This analysis measured the \VH{} cross section to a 4.5$\sigma$ over the background-only hypothesis. The CMS collaboration also released a measurement of the \VH{} production cross section in the \hww{} decay channel using 137 fb$^{-1}$ of data at $\com = 13$ TeV, with a significance of 4.7$\sigma$~\cite{CMS:2021ixs}.

In this thesis a measurement of the corresponding inclusive production cross sections with the \hww{} decay will be performed using $pp$ collisions at $\com = 13.6$ TeV. Additionally a differential cross-section measurement will be performed as a function of the transverse momentum of the associated vector bosons, \ptv{}. Three \ptv{} bins will be used: $0 < \ptv < 75$, $75 < \ptv < 150$, and $\ptv > 150$ GeV. Finally, the Run 2 and Run 3 measurement will be combined to extract the inclusive \VH{} with \hww{} decay cross section.

The analysis covered in this thesis features events with three (3$\ell$) or four (4$\ell$) charged leptons (electrons or muons) in the final state for the \wh{} and \zh{} channels respectively. In the previous Run 2 analysis, the $2\ell$ channel was included, but was found to be systematically limited and expected to not provide any additional sensitivity in this analysis and is thus not included. Leptonic decays of $\tau$ leptons originating from the Higgs boson or associated vector boson are considered as signal since the corresponding electrons or muons will be reconstructed, while hadronically decaying $\tau$ leptons are considered as background. Additionally, events from all other Higgs production mechanism are treated as background. 

The 3$\ell$ channel targets \wh{} production, and is separated into two orthogonal channels: the \zdom{} channel which contains at least one same flavor opposite sign (SFOS) lepton pair, and the \zdep{} channel which has no SFOS lepton pair. Similarly, the 4$\ell$ channel is split into two channels: one with a single SFOS lepton pair, and one with two SFOS lepton pairs.

The rest of the Run 3 analysis will primarily focus on the 3$\ell$ channel, with a brief summary of the 4$\ell$ channel.
