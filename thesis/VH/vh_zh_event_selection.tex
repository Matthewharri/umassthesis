As outlined earlier, events in the \zh{} channel are required to contain exactly four isolated leptons with $\pt > 10$~GeV. The leading lepton must satisfy $\pt > 27$~GeV, the total charge of the system must be zero, and at least one lepton must match the triggering object. In addition, events must include at least one SFOS lepton pair, with all SFOS pairs required to have an invariant mass greater than 12~GeV. For events containing a DFOS pair, the invariant mass of that pair must exceed 10~GeV, suppressing contributions from $Z\gamma^{*}$ and $Z+$quarkonium processes.

To reduce top-quark backgrounds, a $b$-jet veto is applied, requiring no $b$-tagged jets with $\pt > 20$~GeV. Fake-lepton backgrounds are further suppressed by imposing a minimum angular separation of $\Delta R > 0.1$ between \lzero{} and \lone{}. In the two-SFOS-pair channel, orthogonality with analyses targeting $H \to ZZ^{*} \to 4\ell$ is ensured by vetoing events where the four-lepton invariant mass falls within 115-130~GeV. Finally, the invariant mass of the Higgs-associated lepton pair is required to be between 10~GeV and 50~GeV, a selection that reduces the $ZZ^{*}$ background contribution in this category by approximately 87\%. By inverting this cut, a $ZZ^{}$-enriched control region is defined to help constrain this dominant background during the statistical analysis.
