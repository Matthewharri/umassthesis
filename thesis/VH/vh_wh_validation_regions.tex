The \wh{} channel has three additional VR's used solely to assess the agreement between data and MC simulation. The first, the \zdom{} VR, lies in a region of phase space with negligible signal contamination and serves as a sideband to the SR\@. It follows the same selection as the \zdom{} SR, except that at least one SFOS lepton pair must have an invariant mass below 12~GeV. The other two VRs are designed to check the modeling of top backgrounds: the \zdom{}-top and \zdep{}-top VRs. Each uses the same selection as its corresponding SR, except for requiring at least one $b$-tagged jet in the event. These are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:vh_wh_validation_regions}.
