Work is currently ongoing to implement the Gen 3 geometry for the muon system. Once finished, the focus will shift to implementing the frustum octree approach and ensuring this works with the Gen 3 geometry model. Once both tasks are completed, our focus will shift to implementing the first steps of the muon reconstruction chain, segment finding/fitting. The reconstruction chain is entirely dependent on the muon tracking geometry and thus is a bottleneck for developing track reconstruction algorithms, and as such, is a high priority task to finish.
